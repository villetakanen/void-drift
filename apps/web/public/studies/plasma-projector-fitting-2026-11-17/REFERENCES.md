# 02.4 visual reference sources

Retrieved 17 November 2026. The two third-party images are unaltered reference
evidence for visual criticism in the fitting study, not game artwork or assets
to promote into production. Their original credit marks remain in the images.

- `024-doom-plasma-rifle.jpg`: **DOOM Eternal — Plasma Rifle**, model and textures
  by Neil McKnight, concept art by Jon Lane; id Software / Bethesda.
  [Artist's project](https://neilmcknight.artstation.com/projects/lxr1yO).
  [Source image](https://cdna.artstation.com/p/assets/images/images/029/792/280/large/neil-mcknight-plasma-base-01.jpg?1598643639).
- `024-halo-plasma-pistol.jpg`: **Halo Infinite — Plasma Pistol**, high-resolution
  model by Michael Frank; 343 Industries / Microsoft.
  [Artist's project](https://mikejohnfrank86.artstation.com/projects/WmdrxN).
  [Source image](https://cdna.artstation.com/p/assets/images/images/045/069/408/large/michael-frank-2.jpg?1641848667).

The study's observations about form and possible transfer are interpretations,
not statements by these artists. These sources do not establish Void Drift's
weapon mechanics or the recognisability of a future fitting.
