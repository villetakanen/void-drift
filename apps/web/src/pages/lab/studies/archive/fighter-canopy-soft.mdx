# 02.6 / Soft canopy treatments — rejected

**Rejected.** The page has no date. Git shows it was added on 30 October 2026, in
commit `d1484e84`.

## Outcome

All six treatments were rejected. The soft glass and glow did not fit the game's
crisp colors and heavy accents. The low-poly hull and the canopy's forward
position remain agreed; no canopy treatment was accepted.

## Ruled out

Three canopy shapes, each in two finishes:

- Lens: a smooth tapered lens along the spine.
- Kite: a single clean pointed pane that echoes the hull.
- Visor: a short broad pane makes the flight deck more visible.

Ice glass (A Lens, B Kite, C Visor): a cool reflection keeps the canopy within
the ship palette. Amber glow (D Lens, E Kite, F Visor): warm light separates the
canopy from the hull.
