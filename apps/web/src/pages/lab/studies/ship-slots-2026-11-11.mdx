## 01 / A slot for a part outside the scored rules

In the [Hangar](/hangar), Plan view marks each mount with a slot showing what it accepts, whether it is filled and whether it is selected. A filled slot has a lime border and type label on a dark fill. With Playground on, a part outside the scored rules can be fitted, but the run is unscored. With Playground off, a fitted part remains and blocks every launch. Both looked like any other filled slot; unavailable launch buttons gave no reason, so the player had to inspect mounts individually.

The Fiddler has nine slots. A part can extend beyond its slot; Plan view shrank weapons and utilities to fit, a defect outside this study. The existing geometry, empty, filled and selected states, Plan-only display, Playground behaviour and unselectable linked second engine were fixed. The question covered selected and unselected slots and linked mounts with Playground on and off. The [part rows](/lab/studies/part-rows-2026-11-11), [terminology](/lab/studies/run-status-2026-11-10) and unsanctioned ships were outside scope. The [question review](/lab/reviews/ship-slots-2026-11-11) concerns an earlier draft; its corrected text was not retested.

## 02 / Marking the whole Fiddler

<h3 id="02-1">02.1 / A corner for the class</h3>

On 11 November the study selected a filled corner in the slot border's colour for a part outside the scored rules. With Playground on the corner is caution yellow and the slot remains lime. With Playground off the corner, border, type label and linked pair's connector are red; selection corners remain lime. A corner triangle is a stock web pattern the game otherwise avoids, but was accepted here. The red slot did not yet identify the part blocking launch. The thin border alone was insufficient to carry a state, and parts covered the slot fill.

<h3 id="02-2">02.2 / A heavier border</h3>

The unjudged proposal kept the corner and colour rule, but made a blocking slot's red border as heavy as the selection corners, dashed on the linked engine. It asked whether the three blocking slots would be seen first and whether lime selection corners around a red border still read as selection. The heavy border was drawn over the part and could cross a barrel extending beyond the slot; the blocked engine's selection glow remained lime. The ships were larger than in 02.1, nearer Hangar size. This proposal was not judged before the study stopped.
