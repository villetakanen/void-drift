# Initials

<p className="initials-lede">Arcade-style entry for a player's tag.</p>

<div className="initials-stage">
  <div className="panel initials-card">
    <Initials host={2} client:load />
  </div>
</div>

Used in the run-complete dialog, for the tag shown next to a score on Top Scores.

Letters are typed or picked with the arrow keys: Up and Down change the letter, Left and Right move between slots. For the host it is one text field with a `label` and a `value`.

| Prop | Default | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `label` | `"Initials"` | The label under the slots, and the field's accessible name. |
| `host` | `0` | The elevation of the surface the field sits on: `0`, `1` or `2`. |
| `disabled` | `false` | A placeholder: same size, slots dimmed, no focus or input. |

A host moves focus into the field with `focus()`, which selects the first empty slot.

## Keys

One text field holds up to three letters, A–Z, filled from the left. It starts
empty, and each empty slot shows a dim A.

| Key | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Up, Down | Next or previous letter, wrapping at A and Z. On an empty slot the first press enters A. |
| Left, Right | Select the previous or next slot. Right stops at the first empty slot. |
| A letter | Enters it in uppercase and selects the next slot. On a filled slot it replaces the letter. |
| Backspace | On the empty slot or the last letter, removes the last letter. On an earlier letter, selects the previous slot. |
| Delete | Removes the last letter when it is selected. |
| Home, End | Select the first slot, or the last selectable one. |
| Tab, Shift+Tab | Select the next or previous slot. From the last or first slot, leave the field. |
| Paste | Enters the pasted letters, at most three. Other characters are dropped. |

## Elevation

Empty slots and the selected slot are black on every host. A slot that holds a letter and is not selected is one elevation step above its host.

## Disabled

A disabled field holds the place of the field while the host is not ready for entry. It keeps its size, shows its value dimmed and takes no focus or input.

## Accessibility

- One native text field with one label, "Initials" by default. Tab stops at
  each selectable slot before it leaves, so passing the field takes up to
  three presses.
- Accepted loss of accessibility: Tab moving inside a single text field
  departs from standard text-field behaviour, and from the ARIA practice of
  one tab stop per composite control with arrow keys inside. Focus is not
  trapped (WCAG 2.1.2 holds).
- A polite live region announces the letter after each Up or Down.
- Accepted loss of accessibility: the only focus cue is a colour change of
  the selected slot's brackets, from the resting bracket colour to the selected
  colour. Nothing moves and nothing is added. The brackets are 2px lines set
  3px in from the slot's edge in every state. The selected colour is at 10.0:1
  against the resting bracket colour and 18.4:1 against the black slot ground
  of a selected slot; the resting bracket colour is at 1.8:1 against a black
  slot and at 1.7:1, 1.5:1 and 1.3:1 against a raised slot on host 0, 1 and 2.
  This is less
  than WCAG 2.2 Focus Appearance (2.4.13) and the design system's focus ring
  provide: the brackets are open across the middle 40% of the top and bottom,
  and they mark one slot, covering less than a third of a 2px line around the
  field.
