## Outcome / Ignition

**B / Ignition** was accepted: bold Saira Stencil, Android green with a hot core, a flickering strike at each digit's onset, centred over the full-size ship, with no launch ring. Whether the final mark is GO or 0 remained open; the reference uses GO. The original page's last change was 8 November 2026 (Git history; formatting only).

The countdown gives a short, challenging run a deliberate three-second threshold for orientation to the planet and drift. The earlier Launch transition cross-faded to live flight in **0.3 seconds**. The count is status information, not an action cue: Android green is a status colour, lime marks actions and effects inks are transient light. It is painted on the effects surface, where additive stamps and shared decay create afterglow, rather than drawn as HUD text.

*Original accepted reference, captured from the demo at 1280 px: 1920 × 1080 backing canvas, still camera, no randomness. A 0.6 s lead precedes one digit per second. Each digit is stamped for 0.8 s at 0.45 intensity; the strike lasts 0.28 s with a deterministic on/off/half pattern every 1/30 s and an 18 px slip on half steps. The final mark is stamped for 0.4 s at 0.6. The glyph is 420 px at that backing, cached with a 48 px halo, ink body and 30% core. These two stills do not establish the flicker's motion; motion evidence is unavailable in this static record.*

Each variant used production `FxSurface`: cached luminous glyphs stamped each frame and faded by shared decay. A tested bare surface fade, B a neon strike, C motion turned into an echo by persistence; the final mark could release a ring. Ink, typeface, final mark, placement and backdrop were independent switches. The scene used the production starfield and Wasp but no simulation or moving camera. It could not establish whether three seconds feels long over repeated runs; that needs a flight trial.

## Start flow and open choices

`FlightDeck` already had a starting phase: **150 ms** fade to void, handoff to a fresh survival run, then **150 ms** reveal. Input, resources and simulation clock waited for reveal. The proposal extends the wait by three seconds with the revealed run still; Flight Lab and builder flights skip the starting phase. `/lab/landing` composes the production launcher and handoff.

Provisional delivery choices were GO as the final mark; control live with the mark; a thrust key held through the count firing immediately; hidden page or blur pausing the count like the handoff; reduced motion starting immediately with no count. None was considered a delivery blocker. Restarts and audio were outside this cycle.

## Delivery map

A presentation clock was needed because `FlightFx` advances its surface using simulation ticks, which remain zero during the count. Count time during countdown, then count time plus ticks, keeps it monotonic. World-space stamping at the starting position with inverse zoom preserves screen size and leaves the final light at the launch point once the camera follows.

The proposal ships **Saira Stencil 700** outlines for **0–3, G and O** with the OFL licence, generated with `scripts/convert-logomark.py`, not the study font. A Path2D stamp cache replaces the woff2. `--color-android-green` gains a canvas mirror under the existing parity check; the design-system core typeface table gains a row. No `docs/design.md` change was proposed.

The first slice covered the cached ignition painter, colour mirror and glyphs, with descending digits, deterministic strike output, no stamps outside active windows and essential budget use. The second extended `FlightDeck` after reveal, kept simulation and resources still until the final mark, added the presentation clock and preserved world-positioned light. Proposed checks covered repeated activation, hidden-page pause, immediate reduced-motion start and unchanged Lab and builder flights. Fidelity required matching the reference frames and inspecting the real run at 1280 px. The original map named `docs/specs/sfx-canvas.md`, `docs/specs/design-system-core.md` and the landing section of `docs/specs/playable-alpha.md` as spec destinations.
