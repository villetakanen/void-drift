**Accepted by 31 October 2026.** The study settled four engine appearances and their editor and flight fitting treatments. The date comes from Git history: its first registry entry, in `a86640f9`, already records the work as integrated. Performance values were a first proposal for player testing.

## Question

How can low-poly engine bodies express families, subtypes and architectural tradeoffs, both in the catalog and fitted to a ship?

The bodies should be recognizable when switched off, through silhouette, proportion and facets rather than labels, color or plumes. The accepted material direction is dark charcoal with faceted shading; the accent sits at the emission end.

## Four accepted bodies

**Small and Heavy ion** share a tapered needle casing, axial facets, a recessed end and a narrow blue slit. The 02.4 / 02 medium reference became Small ion; 02.4 / 03 became Heavy. Heavy is **115% as long and 145% as wide** as that reference. The smaller 02.4 / 01 was not selected and Medium did not become a third product.

## Fitting and selection

Shrinking an engine into its slot lost its character. The approved editor treatment shows the full-size engine above a **70% opaque hull**, with soft backlight on the selected part. Slots keep a fixed **side → aft → hull/Q** order: background first, then part and highlight, then border and labels. Selection does not reorder them.

![Fighter editor showing selected full-size engine](/studies/engine-identities/selector-1-small-ion.png)

*The Fighter editor treatment with Small ion selected. Captured 18 November 2026 from the built study using then-current code; a reconstruction.*

In flight, Fighter side engines sit below the hull and aft engines above; the study moved the aft pair **five model units** toward the cockpit. Scout engines all sit below the hull in flight, aligned from the slot’s top edge; slot 04 moves **three model units aft** and the linked connector runs above the ship. For the battleship trial, side slots move **one model unit inward** and turn **five degrees farther outward**, while the aft slots move **three model units toward the cockpit**. The rectangular nose support was removed. The readiness section records the Fighter, Scout and battleship treatments as approved.

## Ruled out and deferred

The first family comparison showed the families could be distinct, but its styling was not accepted. The first plasma bodies were also rejected: their vessels looked toy-like and their coil channels too long. The later armored containment was preferred but too detailed, leading to the simplified 02.9 form. Plume aesthetics and final balance remained follow-up work.

## Where the work went

The page records local implementation in the shared builder and custom flight, including engine artwork, mounting, editor treatment and per-mount timing. The first balance proposal kept Heavy ion’s powered ceiling at **750**. Fusion was implemented with a pulse and recovery; Plasma charged before sustained thrust and reset its charge on release or power loss. These were trial behaviors, not final balance. Version 2 replaced version-1 ship storage as requested. Public deployment was separate; the [builder and test flight](/hangar) provide the integrated trial.
