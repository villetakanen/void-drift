**Accepted · 31 October 2026.** The Integrated trident hull and a compact four-mount arrangement were approved. The date comes from Git history: the first registry entry, in `a86640f9`, already records the study as integrated.

## Question

How can a compact, tightly fitted Scout read as an angry little spacecraft rather than a miniature paper-plane Fighter?

## Accepted direction

The Scout should feel tightly packed and fast, with a protruding light-bulb cockpit and fittings crowded around it. The accepted **02.5 / 06 Integrated trident** has flat facets, swept outer knives and a continuous pointed central keel. Its cockpit is black and round, with a collar. The ship keeps its required side-engine pair, one deck mount and one aft mount.

## Ruled out

The leading cockpit was chosen over the deck-leading alternative. The [rounded bodies](/lab/studies/archive/scout-firefly-rounded-bodies) were rejected while the protruding cockpit was retained.

**04 / Split knives** was close in its sweep, proportions and feeling of speed, but its diamond tail read as a separate ornament. Six later wing and tail variants were judged worse, and a joined-neck comparison did not advance the study. The inquiry returned to whole-ship concepts, leading to the Integrated trident.

## What remained uncertain

Visual approval did not establish fitted-part clearance or handling. The inner side mounts slightly overlap and approach the cockpit collar. The study identified possible rail-barrel conflicts, an aft nozzle over the keel, plume clearance and the fairness of a circular collision envelope as questions for review.

## Where the work went

The page records local integration of the Trident hull, cockpit, approved side pair and reconstructed H/A coordinates in the shared builder and flight systems. All three chassis became selectable and saved IDs and builds were preserved. The Scout gun used a mount-relative barrel treatment to clear the cockpit; the aft engine became a surface mount without a keel notch. Human review of fidelity, close-packed fittings, plume appearance and handling remained pending. The [builder and test flight](/hangar) show the integrated Scout.

The full study before conversion is in commit `bcc2065a`, at `apps/web/src/pages/lab/studies/scout-firefly.astro`, with its deleted study files `apps/web/src/components/studies/ScoutAggressiveHulls.astro`, `ScoutJoinedStern.astro`, `ScoutPackingComparison.astro`, `ScoutSplitKnifeVariants.astro`, `ScoutTridentPlacement.svelte`, `ScoutWholeShipConcepts.astro` and `scout-trident-reference.ts` in the same directory.
