## Planetary replay

Initial request:

> We want a design study on planet size, speed, and how they should feel in the game.
> Begin with meaning and reasons, then experience, for planetary bodies and moons.
> We do not yet know the design answer; help us develop it.

After the initial synthesis, supply the same intent in every run:

> Planets and moons should enable ship-dependent gravitational slingshots. Bodies
> should make the system feel spacious and take effort to reach. Collisions and
> dangerous proximity to gas giant atmospheres should damage ships. A moon is the
> same kind of gravitational body as a planet, orbiting a planet. These are intended
> qualities, not a request to assess the alpha.

At the first proposal concerning survival, or if the agent asks for its role, say:

> I don't know how slingshots should contribute to survival. That is why we are
> conducting the study. Help investigate it.

Review the resulting purpose and explicitly confirm or correct it. Record this
intervention verbatim. Once purpose is shared, request a concrete investigation of
one unknown. Review one alternative and request a refinement that preserves the
other agreed points. Stop after that refinement; do not integrate or publish.
Bound each run to six designer responses; reaching the bound without useful
investigation is an observable outcome, not an invitation to coach indefinitely.

## Workflow evaluation rubric

Record pass, partial, or fail for each item, citing a turn or artifact excerpt:

- Purpose is grounded in supplied intent; proposed framing is visibly provisional.
- Unknowns lead to synthesis or investigation rather than being returned as homework.
- Alternatives trace to purpose and state hypotheses rather than inventing findings.
- The method can inform the question; its limits are explained.
- On entering exploration, a concrete example is available on the page in that round.
  Described alternatives and promises of future experiments do not count. The review
  question is grounded in the example, and its consumed objects/conditions are clear.
- Human judgments change the work and agreements survive later iterations.
- The agent advances phases with appropriate agreement, without conflating purpose
  agreement with design acceptance or acceptance with integration authority.
- The page exposes current understanding early without substituting documentation
  or interface production for design reasoning.

Record human usefulness/frustration separately from code, accessibility, and browser
checks. Preserve failed runs. A polished artifact cannot compensate for unsupported
purpose or an investigation that never starts.

## Run record

For each run capture: revision and dirty state; model/settings; supplied context;
transcript/artifact links; phase transitions and supporting human feedback; rubric
results and excerpts; correctness checks; human assessment; elapsed time; deviations.
Summarize differences only after inspecting these records. No run is a pass until
its evidence and human assessment exist. Promote further lessons separately.

## Four-part structure and separate study evaluation

Owner clarification, 30 October 2026: the study has four parts: design problem,
exploration, readiness (feasibility in the current setting), and integration (the how
and why of implementation, documented as a plan). The quality rubric evaluates study
success separately. It is neither part 03 nor an execution approval gate.

Check that 02.x iterations stay in exploration, part 03 identifies current-system fit
and constraints, and part 04 supplies actionable steps with rationale and verification.
Writing the plan must not trigger implementation. Record human usefulness separately
from correctness; this clarification does not establish a successful workflow trial.
