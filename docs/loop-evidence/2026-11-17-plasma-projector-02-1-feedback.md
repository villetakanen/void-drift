# Plasma Projector 02.1 feedback · 17 November 2026

The designer found the example useful because placing the projector's plasma
beside the plasma engine made their family relationship clear. This is the
retained result; it is not acceptance of the whole weapon or its contours.

The designer observed that carrying material from a stopped study can remove
drift while also losing useful discoveries, that plasma belongs mainly on FX
and may be difficult to express against movement, and that the initial plume
direction and shape cannot serve as the weapon's model. The proposed working
sequence is loading; a huge explosion with strong backward kick; then cooling
with a smaller, longer backward thrust. Losing the target would leave loading
and cooling only.

The designer subsequently clarified that these are contributions to a design
study, not a recipe to implement literally. They also saw hallmarks of the
previous unsuccessful correction loop in 02.1 and suggested lost context and
over-correction after undetected drift as possible contributors. No cause is
established. This record changes no skill, standing process or project spec.

The designer identified the earlier failure pattern more specifically: attempts
alternated between too closely reproducing schematic conceptual vectors and
trying something entirely different, repeatedly asking for feedback on minutiae.
They read 02.1 as approaching that failure point, rather than recovering the
earlier study's useful findings. This is their assessment, not evidence that a
particular technical cause has been established.

02.1 retains four frames captured before changing its instrument, with settings
and evidence limits on the study page. 02.2 kept the plasma-family comparison
and investigated whether loading, release and cooling read as one committed
action. The encounter and force-reference options inspect one whole proposal;
its form and fixed recipe remained open to judgment.

Subsequent decision: 02.2 established continuous backward thrust during venting
as a study constraint. In 02.3, both vent forms remained promising, and the
designer identified provisional equipment form and mounting as the missing
reference for judging the effects. The study is parked as blocked on that
separate equipment-form study. The study page retains the decision and captures;
this does not establish a general cause for earlier failures.
