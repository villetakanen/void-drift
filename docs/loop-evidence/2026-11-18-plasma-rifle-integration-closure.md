# Plasma rifle integration closure · 18 November 2026

**Disposition.** The designer rejected the candidate and requested deletion. No feature from this attempt was delivered to the shared checkout. The [operation study](2026-11-18-plasma-rifle-operation.md) and [earlier integration retrospective](../plans/2026-11-18-plasma-integration-retrospective.md) describe separate loops.

**Scope.** The goal was a playable Playground rifle based on the original study, developed in a detached worktree at RC1 `66391118`. The designer clarified that the small flight example was an early checkpoint toward the whole feature. A committed spray should erupt even on a clear barrel line. A fragment entering detached hot fumes should take a small hit even after the ports close. Enemy planning, backend and release changes were outside scope.

**Flight evidence.** The implementer and independent reviewer reported two controlled Wasp passes through the production flight path. The first setup produced a direct hit. In the corrected pass, the spray reached its 78-unit endpoint in empty space and a nearby wreck took 50 damage. In the loss pass, a fragment entered hot spray after the ports closed, took 3 damage and survived. The controlled driver supplied encounter positions and fragment entry. The implementer also reported a fitted Fighter firing alongside an ion cannon in Gauntlet.

**Appearance and targeting.** At the checkpoint the implementer described the Wasp charge as hard to see and the spray as smoother and redder than the study reference. Work continued. After viewing the candidate, the designer said “it doesn't look like what we had in the study.” Subsequent source inspection found rail acquisition still scanning the full physical body list in the candidate. The specific rail correction described in the earlier retrospective was absent.

**Verification reported during the run.** On the uncommitted candidate the implementer reported 278 passing unit tests, passing TypeScript, Astro, Svelte and lint checks, a successful 111-page static build and a clean `git diff --check`. Ten-second headed Chromium samples on an M4 Max reported a 9.2 ms p95 frame interval with the rifle idle, one firing and two firing among 24 additional fragments. The last sample peaked at 17.6 ms. The idle comparison used the candidate with its fitted rifle held in cooldown.

**Cleanup and evidence limits.** The preview was stopped and the detached worktree removed at the designer’s request. The subsequent worktree listing confirmed removal. The candidate, recordings, traces and check logs were deleted. The check results and measurements above come from reports in the development chat; exact verification invocations and the final dirty tree cannot be inspected from this record. No local commit, push, deployment or hosted verification occurred. Closure did not rerun the checks.
