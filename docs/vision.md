# Void Drift

Void Drift explores a medium: the small, neat ship duel of Star Control 2's
Super Melee, remade for today with the ship design of Master of Orion. A fast,
explosive, unpredictable fly-and-shoot game, where a fight is decided by physics
and by the ship you brought.

The player is a rival among rivals. Anything in the game earns its place by
sharpening a rivalry: a run, a score, a ship, a fight. It should delight its
maker, hook a stranger with a link, satisfy arcade veterans and show builders
how it was made.

It is built by conversation, so the game is also a harness for making games
with agents. Its lasting value is learning material for the agents and people
who come after.
