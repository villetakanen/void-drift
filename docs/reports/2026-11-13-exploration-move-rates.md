# Exploration moves in past studies: counts and rates

Status: evidence record, 2026-11-13. Kept out of the exploration guide on purpose
(decided 2026-11-16 in conversation, for now): the numbers are weaker than they look
and would read as a ranking. The guide's "Choose the move" section maps the state of
the question to a move and does not depend on them.

## Method

- 284 part-02 steps from 44 study pages were indexed and labelled by move and by
  result. Labelling was one pass by Haiku and was not audited.
- 262 statements on moves that helped or hurt were collected from 12 reviews and 70
  loop-evidence records by Sonnet. Five quotes were spot-checked.

## Share of steps that ended with something selected or fixed

| Move                         | Rate | Steps |
| ---------------------------- | ---- | ----- |
| Hand over the control        | 75%  | 24    |
| Ladder                       | 73%  | 23    |
| Settle the foundation        | 70%  | 30    |
| Put it where it lives        | 59%  | 22    |
| Spread                       | 58%  | 74    |
| Narrow                       | 57%  | 57    |
| Run the real system          | 54%  | 22    |
| Single first example         | 42%  | 14    |
| Start from an accepted thing | none | 7     |
| Assemble                     | none | 6     |

Accepted studies settle something in 71% of steps, stopped studies in 33%. Half of
the accepted studies end on handing over the control or putting the result where it
lives.

## Limits

- Study pages keep only retained steps; rejected rounds were deleted. Every rate
  understates failure, by an amount that may differ per move.
- The rates are associations. A study with an unanswerable question produces the
  same pattern whatever moves are chosen.
- Two moves, "Combine" and "Next dependent decision", were identified by reading and
  had no label in the pass. Each rests on a handful of steps. They are left out of
  the guide for now.
- The guide does not tell agents to prefer a spread to a single first example. The
  42% rests on 14 steps.
