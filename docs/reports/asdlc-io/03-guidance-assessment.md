# Assessment against ASDLC.io guidance

**Overall assessment:** Void Drift demonstrates substantial, intentional use of ASDLC's spec-anchored context, shared definitions, executable checks and governed learning. The strongest inspectable alignment is in the structure of contracts and verification evidence. The record is less conclusive about improved design inquiry, reliable critic judgment, or cumulative gains from amended procedures. Its adaptations expose useful distinctions for the framework; they are not a project remediation list.

This is a qualitative case assessment, not a certification or compliance percentage. Status labels below are the captured pages' own labels; they do not establish empirical validation. Guidance IDs resolve through the three [guidance packets](01-method-and-sources.md#guidance-register); project IDs resolve through the [project packets](01-method-and-sources.md#project-evidence-register).

## Where the case adds most

The shared vocabulary is useful: contracts, context routing, distinct gate types and governed learning all describe real mechanisms or decisions here. Its main documentary contribution is a richer account of boundaries: inquiry versus assembly, procedure versus enforcement, adopted versus effective, and accepted property versus integrated/verified/released artifact. [Findings](05-findings-for-asdlc.md) develop these into bounded claims.

The sources also reveal a directly inspectable guidance inconsistency: the [Getting Started](https://asdlc.io/getting-started/) and [Double Diamond](https://asdlc.io/patterns/agentic-double-diamond/) source-code metaphor conflicts with the explicit spec-anchored boundary in [The Spec](https://asdlc.io/patterns/the-spec/). This conclusion comes from comparing current guidance, not from attributing a project failure to that wording.

No examined evidence establishes that ASDLC caused the observed successes or failures. The project deliberately used the guidance, but shares authorship context with it, evolved multiple procedures concurrently, and lacks controlled comparisons. The strongest conclusion is usefulness and limits of explanatory fit in this case, with concrete proposals for clearer documentation.
