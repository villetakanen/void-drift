# Learning and historical outcomes evidence

## Scope, identity and coverage

At baseline commit `49336a6e5675da1b50b7179f3baaf28f6d3a7bb4` (observed `HEAD`), the working tree had untracked assessment-plan/report material; no modified tracked paths were reported. This packet and inventory exclude those untracked files and use committed source content at the baseline. Collection is limited to the project’s loop records and learning/retrospective documents; it is not a full factory history or an effectiveness evaluation.

The companion [`loop-inventory.json`](loop-inventory.json) inventories **69 committed, top-level Markdown records** under `docs/loop-evidence/`. Each entry records path, SHA-256, filename/date, H1 name, every heading with source line, and any explicit Date/Status/Outcome/Disposition fields found in the first 35 lines. Nested artifact logs/images are excluded. Date is taken from filenames, not independently established event dates. This is metadata coverage, not semantic review of 69 episodes; sparse/missing status fields do not establish missing outcomes. No frequency or success rate is inferred.

I inspected the close-loop skill plus the learning index and lesson 002, and sampled eight records selected after inventory for early/recent coverage, explicit stopped/failed/implemented outcomes, a factory amendment, subsequent learning adoption, and an ambiguous/latest delivery. Sample records sometimes describe distinct iterations of a larger topic; they are not independent confirmations of a common cause. Retrospective digests are secondary and checked against the sampled primary loop records where possible.

## Cross-record learning/adoption observations

The close-loop skill (`.agents/skills/close-loop/SKILL.md`, sections **Scope and sources**, **Record**, **Interpretation boundary**, **Completion**) defines evidence collection as separate from signal/lesson derivation, requires provenance and gaps, and bars promoting a finding to a cause or standing change inside closure. It therefore supports interpreting the 69 files as evidence records, not as 69 successful loops or as validated lessons.

`docs/factory-building-lessons/README.md` says lesson records are not automatically instructions and completion does not imply empirical validation. `002-phased-design-studies.md` (especially **Done tasks and evidence**, **Evidence and interpretation**, and dated **Adopted** sections) records adoption through skill/spec changes and links implementation to basis. It also repeatedly marks effectiveness untested. Example: after 29 October failures, a phased study workflow and an inspectable-example rule were adopted; later 2 November utilities failed, with failure notes emphasizing that no causal explanation was established. The pattern is evidence of documented adoption and continued failure, not proof the change caused or failed to prevent it. The 6 November derelict correction-loop analysis is explicitly proposed and not adopted, while 11 November writing/closure guidance records a new individual approval without fresh efficacy evidence.

The 8 November secondary retrospective digest (`docs/reviews/2026-11-08-retro-0-6-0/factory-lessons.md`, sections 1–5) is useful for candidate chronology and contradictions, but it draws on external-to-repository memory files and itself flags unresolved tensions. It should not be treated as independent confirmation of the primary loop records. No controlled before/after comparison, replay of comparable study prompts, model comparison, or sample-wide semantic audit was performed. The sample is purposive, small, and skewed toward explicit failure or lesson labels; unselected material may change interpretation. Generalization beyond this project is unresolved.
