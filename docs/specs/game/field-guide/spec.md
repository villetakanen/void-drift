# Field Guide

## Blueprint

### Context

The Field Guide is Void Drift’s public encyclopedia: a place to look up how the
game works, follow an interesting link, or learn about the game before playing.
It is a reading destination on the game’s website, separate from the playable
game and the developer-facing [Lab](../../lab/spec.md). This contract covers
publishing, browsing, and discovery. The article list and individual articles
are separate editorial work; Credits is the one known migration.

### Architecture

- `/field-guide` is the browsing index. `/field-guide/<slug>` is the permanent
  address of an article. The index lists each published article by title with
  its summary; each article links to the index and the game’s title screen.
  Articles can link directly to one another using ordinary links.
- An Astro content collection of repository-authored MDX files is the source
  for articles. The collection schema requires a title, summary, and stable,
  unique URL slug. Astro builds the index and article HTML from the collection.
- Article frontmatter uses the same provenance fields as the specs:
  `generated.by` identifies the producer (`producer/version` or `human:<id>`)
  and `generated.at` records when the article was generated or last
  substantively edited. `verified` is `[]` or contains a single record with
  `by: human:<id>` and `at` set to the actual confirmation timestamp. Both
  timestamps use RFC 3339 with a timezone and are compared as instants.
- Below the article body, a visible provenance block shows “Generated by” with
  the producer and date. It also shows “Verified by a human” with the verifier’s
  public name or handle and date only when `verified.at` is at or after
  `generated.at`. An older record can remain in frontmatter, but the current
  article is unverified. The block is part of the page HTML.
- The index and article pages each have a descriptive document title, a meta
  description, and an absolute canonical URL on the public site. The public
  sitemap includes the index and published article URLs.
- Credits lives at `/field-guide/credits` and carries forward the attribution
  and licensing information at the existing `/credits` page. `/credits`
  permanently redirects to the new address so old links still work.

### Constraints

- Readers can open, read, and navigate the encyclopedia without signing in,
  starting the game, or enabling JavaScript.
- Publishing is repository-managed. Reader accounts, public editing, and
  encyclopedia search are outside this contract; browsing starts with the
  index and article links.

## Contract

### Definition of Done

- A production build publishes the index and a page for each valid MDX entry.
- The public HTML, links, metadata, sitemap, and Credits redirect satisfy the
  scenarios below.

### Regression guardrails

- Editing an article’s title, summary, or body preserves its URL unless the
  publisher explicitly changes its slug.
- Automated checks and AI review never count as human verification.
- The Field Guide is descriptive; the [game contracts](../spec.md) remain the source
  of truth for gameplay behaviour.
