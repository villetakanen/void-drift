# 0.6.0 "The ion storm"

Scope stated in conversation on 8 November 2026, during the retrospective
[held the same day](../reviews/2026-11-08-retro-0-6-0/README.md). This plan
records what the release needs and what is known to be missing; each piece of
work is planned in its own focused plan. Status on 10 November 2026:
deliverables 1 and 2 committed on `feat/0-5-0`, the latest derelicts work
not yet pushed; deliverable 3
specified, not started; deliverable 4 not started; release not prepared.

## Goal

Polish the game to a state where all the basics are found: three stable
ships, a stable ion option for every basic ship part, scorable runs that
should not need their scores removed again, and the Hangar as the way to choose
what you fly.

## Terms

> **ONLY "ACTIVE SHIP" IS DECIDED. EVERY OTHER TERM HERE IS A PLACEHOLDER, IS
> NOT A DECISION, AND WILL NOT BECOME ONE BY BEING WRITTEN HERE.** Do not copy
> the placeholders into code, copy or new specs as if they were chosen.
- **Stable** (placeholder): a ship chassis or part its developers judge good
  enough for alpha play to be scored. It may still change, but not in ways
  expected to affect scores (10 November 2026). It is a judgement, not a
  quality bar with a checklist. "Production" and "starter" are wrong words for
  this and are not used.
- **Experimental** (placeholder): a chassis or part still in development.
  Usable only with Experimental On, and never scored.
- **Active ship** (chosen 10 November 2026, the term EVE Online uses): the ship
  the player has chosen to fly; the title screen shows it and PRESS START flies
  it.
- **Fitting** (placeholder): the parts installed on a ship.

There is no starter kit and no default fitting (10 November 2026). The Wasp
comes fitted with the light ion cannon, the ion point defence and one light ion
engine for one reason: a new player must be able to press Start without
visiting the Hangar. It is not a suggested build, and nothing in the game
restores it. Other ships may carry helper parts in the catalog data; that is
not a contract. (Later progression may make some schematics available by
default; that is not part of 0.6.0.) In code the Wasp's fitting comes from
`starterLoadout("wasp")`; the function name is not a product concept.

## Stable set

Stable content is scorable. The intent is that scores from 0.6.0 remain
valid; whether `CHALLENGE` changes is decided at release preparation as usual.

| Part        | Stable options                                                                                 | State today                         |
| ----------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| Ships       | Wasp, Scout, Fighter                                                                           | exist; polish to stable quality     |
| Ship option | ion stabilizers                                                                                | exist                               |
| Engines     | light ion (`ion`), heavy ion (`heavy-ion`)                                                     | work                                |
| Weapons     | light ion cannon, rotating ion point-defence system                                            | cannon shipped in 0.5.6; PDS is new |
| Equipment   | Potential Well (`battery`), Heavy Potential Well (`heavy-well`), Gravity coil (`gravity-coil`) | exist                               |

Stabilizers are a ship option, not equipment. Ships have an inbuilt solar
collector; there is no slottable collector equipment yet. Everything else in
the catalogs (Corvette, Fiddler, `fusion`, `plasma`, `rail`, `laser`, the
Loading coil `coil`) is experimental.

## Rules

- Only a run flown with stable ships, options and parts is scored and
  leaderboard eligible. The Experimental toggle enforces this (10 November
  2026): with it Off, only stable content can be fitted and flown, so
  every run that starts is scored.
- With Experimental On, every ship and part is available and no run is scored,
  even one flown with only stable parts. In flight and on run complete the
  experimental notice takes the score's place; the time still shows (10
  November 2026). Experimental is not a game mode of its own.
- Turning Experimental Off removes nothing; experimental content stays fitted,
  disabled, and blocks launch until the active ship and its fitting are all
  stable.
- The experimental state is visible on the front of the game before flight.
- Contract: [Experimental mode spec](../specs/game/experimental/spec.md).

## Deliverables

1. **Rotating ion point-defence system.** The one new equipment of the release.
   Starts as a design study with a stated problem (decided 8 November 2026),
   then spec and implementation.
2. **Derelicts in the main game.** Bring derelicts from the derelicts mode into
   survival ([derelicts spec](../specs/game/derelicts/spec.md)).
3. **Hangar selects the ship.** The main screen shows the ship selected in the
   Hangar, including its fitting, and PRESS START flies it; the Hangar is
   where the player chooses the active ship and fits it. The Experimental toggle enables
   the experimental ships and parts; the front of the game shows when a run
   will not be scored. A draft focused plan, not yet reviewed:
   [Experimental mode and the active setup](2026-11-10-experimental-mode.md).
4. **Polish of the basic flows.** Title, Hangar, flight, death, scores. No
   list yet; collect from play.

## Known gaps (10 November 2026)

What is known to be missing for each part of the goal, from the code, the specs
and the plans as of `d7161cf`. Not a task list or an order; each gap is planned
in a focused session.

### Three stable ships

- The Scout and the Fighter start unfitted in the Hangar (`defaultLoadout`),
  as intended. Required engines are always fitted, so every ship can fly
  ([active ship](../specs/game/active-ship/spec.md)).
- Stable is a judgement by the developers, not a checklist; there is no
  "stable quality" list to write. The [ships spec](../specs/game/ships/spec.md)
  says what a ship is and names none but the Wasp.
- Scores are kept per ship, but today only the title-screen Wasp run is ranked,
  so Scout and Fighter rows are unreachable until the Hangar selects the ship
  (deliverable 3).

### A stable ion option for every basic part

- The stable set exists only in this plan's table: the catalogs
  (`CHASSIS`, `PARTS`) carry no stable or experimental class
  ([Experimental mode](../specs/game/experimental/spec.md) E1).
- With two weapons, the 10% weapon energy floor and light ion at 1.75 E/s, the
  Wasp's fire budget is small; it is to be checked in play, with derelict cells
  as the refill.

### Scorable runs that keep their scores

- Ranking is hard-coded to the stock title run (`!loadout` in
  `FlightDeck.svelte`); it has to follow the Experimental toggle.
- The saved score changes (10 November 2026,
  [scores spec](../specs/game/scores/spec.md)): `seconds` gains milliseconds,
  `score` loses its ceiling, `shipId` accepts any ship with no list in
  `firestore.rules` or the schema, and a required `fitting` list of part ids, with
  `stabilizers` and `linkedEngines` as flown, is
  added for game design, not shown on the board. Today the rules list four
  ships including the Corvette, the schema excludes only Fiddler, `MAX_SCORE`
  is 100000 and no fitting is saved. Only the Experimental setting keeps
  experimental content off the board (E10).
- No `ion-storm` scores are saved yet (stated 10 November 2026), so the
  record can change without migrating rows. One best per player and ship,
  whatever the fitting, is confirmed; with three stable ships the 30-row board
  can show as few as ten players. Decided the same day: `seconds` has no upper
  bound (3600 today), and only the current challenge is writable. The rules
  still accept writes to six earlier boards, each replaced when a release
  changed the rules of play: `alpha-1`, `gravity-1` (29 October), `ion-1`
  (0.4.3), `arrival-1` (0.5.4), `arrival-2` (0.5.5) and `arrival-3` (0.5.6).
  Their rows stay in the database.
- `CHALLENGE` is already `ion-storm`. Any change to scoring or to the stable set
  before release needs a decision on whether it changes again.
- The flight screen and run complete show a score whatever the setting; with
  Experimental On they are to show the experimental notice instead (E7).
- Unchecked in the [game spec](../specs/game/spec.md): run complete explains a
  missing Firebase.
- Lab pages mount the game's flight shell: `/lab/builder` mounts the whole
  Hangar (`ShipBuilder`), and several study pages and `/lab/landing` mount
  `FlightDeck`. They cannot save today only because any loadout is unranked;
  once ranking follows the Experimental toggle, nothing keeps them from Save
  Score. The new [Lab spec](../specs/lab/spec.md) says the Lab has no game
  modes and never submits a score, so `/lab/builder` as a full playable Hangar
  conflicts with it.

### The Hangar as the way to choose what you fly

- PRESS START and the title screen's ship are always the fitted Wasp, whatever
  the Hangar holds ([active ship](../specs/game/active-ship/spec.md) A4).
- The active ship is not stored; the Hangar's ship comes from the `chassis`
  query, which the [Hangar spec](../specs/game/hangar/spec.md) now drops (H5). Fittings
  per ship are stored (A7).
- The Experimental toggle is inert and says "Not available yet": no locking, no
  launch block, no persistence (E2–E7, Hangar H4).
- No experimental marker and no "not ranked" copy exist (E8). Their wording
  needs a design study with a live prototype (E11).
- Browser suites assert today's behaviour and change with it
  (`hangar-opening`, `builder`, `flight-deck`, `scout`, `salvage`,
  `derelicts-score`) as does the Firebase `competition` suite.

### Polish of the basic flows

- No list yet; collected from play.
- Derelicts mode shows a score at game over today (seconds plus kills, in
  `FlightDeck.svelte` and `DerelictsResult`). Decided 10 November 2026: only
  Survival has a score, so that score goes
  ([Derelicts spec](../specs/game/derelicts/spec.md) M5). Game over states
  the time survived and the derelicts destroyed instead, as plain facts.

### Release

- The version is `0.5.6`; there is no 0.6.0 release note, and no full
  verification, prepare, check or publish has run.
- All 0.5.x and 0.6.0 work lives on `feat/0-5-0`, 12 local commits ahead of
  `origin/feat/0-5-0` (from `e93f02e` to `d7161cf`); `main` is far behind.

## Missing specs (10 November 2026)

The deliverables cannot be planned reliably until these contracts exist.
Specs hold the intent; this list only names what is missing.

- **Active ship.** Drafted 10 November 2026 as the
  [active ship spec](../specs/game/active-ship/spec.md). The ship select spec
  (0.4.0, a title-screen selector) described the opposite and is deleted.
- **Ships.** Drafted 10 November 2026 as the
  [ships spec](../specs/game/ships/spec.md): what a hull fixes, the cruise
  ceiling law, energy, stabilizers and linked engines. It absorbs the former
  propulsion spec and lists no ships; the catalog in code is the list.
- **Parts.** Drafted 10 November 2026 as the
  [parts spec](../specs/game/parts/spec.md): the kinds of part, slot
  compatibility and the weapon energy floor. It lists no parts. The light ion
  cannon and the ion point defence keep specs of their own.
- **Choosing and fitting in the Hangar.** Rewritten 10 November 2026 as the
  [Hangar spec](../specs/game/hangar/spec.md); the "preserved assembly
  specification" is gone. Decided the same day: the Hangar opens on the active
  ship and the `chassis` query is dropped; Plan and View, the linked-engines
  choice, flight estimates and the mirrored engine pair stay; parts can be
  fitted in View; there is no reset of a fitting; the Hangar works by touch on
  an iPad in landscape; the flight actions are Practice, Derelicts, Gauntlet
  and Survival, with Survival primary and Gauntlet shown disabled.
- **Modes.** Stated 10 November 2026: Survival is the full game. Practice,
  Derelicts and Gauntlet are each a mode of its own and are not merged into
  Survival or each other. They are not grouped under a shared name: calling
  Derelicts a "practice mode" invites merging it with Practice. Each mode's
  purpose is stated in its own spec only; a spec describes its own feature.
  Derelicts is for shooting: a lot to shoot, nothing shoots back.
- **Gauntlet.** Named 10 November 2026 as a flight action the Hangar shows
  disabled. Beyond the line above, nothing about it is decided, and it has no
  spec.
- **Survival.** Rewritten 10 November 2026 as the
  [Survival spec](../specs/game/survival/spec.md): endings, arrival, hull loss,
  time, derelicts as intent, and one score formula in code. Decided the same
  day: point values and arrival tuning are runtime configuration, not spec;
  the spec does not say whether Survival gets harder over time (not known);
  it does not rule out restoring hull (a support part that trades energy for
  hull is planned, release not decided).
- **Derelicts.** Split and rewritten 10 November 2026. The
  [derelict spec](../specs/game/world/derelict/spec.md) says what a derelict,
  a piece and an energy cell are in any mode. The
  [Derelicts spec](../specs/game/derelicts/spec.md) keeps the mode: entry,
  arrivals, endings, return. Decided the same day: Derelicts is one of the
  further single-player modes beside Survival, with Practice and Gauntlet; it
  has no score; rail gun and laser rules belong in no mode spec. They are now
  in no spec at all: both weapons are experimental, and the parts spec holds
  the rules every weapon shares.

- **Practice and options.** Rewritten 10 November 2026.
  [Practice](../specs/game/test-flight/spec.md) is the mode for flying, where
  the ship cannot be lost. [Options](../specs/game/options/spec.md) holds one
  setting, Invert controls, and no ship choice.
- **Deleted 10 November 2026:** the ship select spec, its `camera-zoom.md` and
  the high scores spec. Initials, the cause of a run's end and anonymous play
  moved to the game spec.
- **Scores.** Drafted 10 November 2026 as the
  [scores spec](../specs/game/scores/spec.md): the ten fields of a saved
  score, one best per player and ship, the board, and the Firestore rules. The
  score rules left the game spec, and `MAX_SCORE` with them.

Still open after the spec session:

- `SettingsSchema` still stores `selectedShipId`; the options spec says
  settings hold no ship choice (O5).
- The "legacy stock" ship has left the ships and Survival specs; the star spec
  still has rules for it.
- Out of scope for 0.6.0: planets, systems, mount planner and the Lab specs.

Covered by current specs: survival score and derelict arrivals
([survival](../specs/game/survival/spec.md)), ranking and the leaderboard
([game](../specs/game/spec.md)), run complete ([game](../specs/game/spec.md),
[dialogs](../specs/game/dialogs/spec.md)), derelicts, the cannon, the point
defence and Experimental mode.
