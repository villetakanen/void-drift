## First playable encounter

A pilot fits the 17.5-mass launcher in Playground, enters Gauntlet and watches a drone leave the rail, coast, ignite and burst beside a moving opponent. A useful first slice connects the saved fitting, actual mount pose, shared weapon budget, encounter body and a single intercept through the normal flight loop. Completion also covers competing mounts, defence, recovery, terrain and carrier loss. The study’s 02.5 flight, 02.9 sparks and 02.10 intercept supply references. The production launch gate is 250 u.

## Architecture review

**Fitting and flight geometry.** Define the part and tuning in the [loadout](../../packages/mode-a/src/lib/schemas/loadout.ts) and [combat schemas](../../packages/mode-a/src/lib/schemas/salvage.ts) first. The preview release class already gates saved fittings through the Hangar and scored-loadout check. Agreed on 2026-11-26: the launcher follows normal [weapon fitting rules](../specs/game/parts/spec.md#architecture). Weapon, equipment and universal slots accept it, including the Fighter’s aft universal slot. Share the launcher master, anchor and muzzle between the card, fitted ship, collision envelope and rail motion. Use production Canvas paths for the drone hull.

**Mount and power.** The [weapon scheduler](../../packages/mode-a/src/lib/salvage.ts) owns each ready mount, its 2-energy debit and 3 s reload. Check the carrier’s three-drone cap again as each funded mount releases. The shared reserve is a prerequisite across weapons. Production still has capacity-dependent floors and an emergency based on fitted kinds. Use each mount’s next debit, including a rifle’s actual charge increment or full-charge release, in the [simulation](../../packages/mode-a/src/lib/simulation.ts). At exactly 12 energy a lone launcher holds fire and the alert stays off. Defence spends first, then guns in slot order. Off blocks launches while airborne drones and paid rifle cycles keep advancing.

**Encounter and targeting.** Simulation owns drones, waves and stable IDs for each body’s entire lifetime. Survival replaces pirates at the same roster index. Reusing that identity could refund a replacement carrier or suppress a wave hit. Join launcher and airborne pursuit requests to the [shared acquisition pass](../../packages/mode-a/src/lib/targeting.ts) across Gauntlet quarter-steps and Survival’s separate player weapon path. Keep the eligible enemy while strictly within 500 u, otherwise select nearest then lower ID. Point defence needs hostile drones to pass its existing shooter filters. Allies can still intercept fire. Preserve the idle fast path and one acquisition visit per body.

**Movement and contact.** Derive ejection from the translating and rotating fitting. Carry muzzle velocity into coast, arm on first positive main thrust, and use Fighter steering and ion thrust as the movement reference. Enemy pursuit takes priority over return. With neither destination in range, brake spin and retain velocity. Returning hull contact consumes the drone and refunds its surviving carrier once. Maintain previous and current geometry for swept encounters, including [remaining derelict material](../../packages/mode-a/src/lib/derelict-collision.ts). The armed 15 u hull fuse protects the carrier inside the radiation radius. Planet contact or the star’s danger zone overrides that protection. Immediate radiation deals 30 damage within 30 u. Each wave remembers which moving bodies it has hit for 3 damage out to 180 u. Depleting a drone’s 4 health removes it without a chain detonation. Independent flight survives carrier loss within an active encounter. [Gauntlet](../specs/game/gauntlet/spec.md) already resolves same-tick deaths and ends on a terminal result.

**Light and timing.** Simulation emits detonation and wave-contact positions. The [flight frame](../../packages/mode-a/src/lib/flight-frame.ts) draws the hull on World, forked green tracks into FX history and pale tips on LFX. Hull hits send the same sparks back towards the burst. Reuse [ion plume drawing](../../packages/core/src/lib/effects/engine-plume.ts), cached stamps, visibility bounds and the shared [FX lifecycle](../specs/factory/effects/spec.md). The approved [Drone Launcher timing](../specs/game/drone-launcher/spec.md#scenarios) follows the [study shell](../../apps/web/src/components/studies/drone-launcher-2026-11-25/fission-light.ts). Combat and rendering share a front that expands linearly from 14 u at detonation to 180 u over 44 simulation ticks at 60 Hz.
