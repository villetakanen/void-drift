# Confirmed follow-up study: stellar variety

Confirmed in the Suns design conversation on 2026-11-07. Not started.

## Dependency and purpose

Deliver the standard Sun first: settled mechanics, HUD and field-line feedback,
and the Suns Lab reference article. Continue from the evidence and independent
field model in `/lab/studies/suns-2026-11-07`; do not reopen its standard-Sun
baseline without an explicit reason.

Then investigate how other regular stellar families vary temperature/colour,
radius, gravity, harvestable radiation, damaging exposure and activity. Define
how the player distinguishes reach from severity, and how those differences
change play. The standard Sun is the comparison reference, not a requirement
that every property scale with its visible radius.

## Boundaries

Other star families are excluded from the current study’s delivery. Quasars,
black holes and fictional exotic objects remain outside this follow-up unless
separately scoped. No follow-up implementation is authorized by this record.

## Current-study collateral to complete first

- Shared standard-Sun collection and damage definitions, preserving gravity tuning.
- Life-support draw 0.8 E/s and Light ion draw 1.0 E/s without handling changes.
- Standard-Sun identity across current game presentations, including landing visuals.
- Reusable energy-collection field configuration and readings in Path analysis.
- HUD and field-line states, and a polished Suns Lab article covering
  fiction, zone boundaries and expected in-game behaviour.

The current study remains open only for bounded HUD and field-line decisions.
Standard-Sun artwork, colours, corona and effects are deferred; they must not
block the mechanics patch and are separate from stellar-variety research. This follow-up is a recorded
commitment, not a scheduled automation or a newly launched design cycle.
