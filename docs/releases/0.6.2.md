# 0.6.2 — Yellow-family stars

Derelicts and Gauntlet use deterministic yellow-family stars with size-related
gravity, collection and damage fields. Practice uses the fixed G7 reference
profile, and the title scene samples a cosmetic yellow-family star. The Lab
records the accepted stellar-character and encounter studies.

Ranked Survival rules and scoring are unchanged: it retains the legacy Sun and
the `ion-storm` leaderboard challenge. The new star rules apply to other modes
and do not reset or close the ranked board. All workspace packages are versioned
0.6.2.
