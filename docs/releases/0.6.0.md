# 0.6.0 — The ion storm

Released 11 November 2026. General polish was closed for this release.

Rotating ion point defence joins the light ion cannon. Derelicts now appear
in Survival, providing targets, physical debris and energy pickups.

The Hangar chooses the active ship and keeps each ship’s fitting. The title
screen and launches use that ship and fitting. Wasp, Scout and Fighter form
the sanctioned ship set. Playground opens the remaining ships and parts and
turns scoring off; switching it off preserves the fitting and blocks launch
until the active build is eligible for ranked play.

Ranked Survival scores record the ship, fitted parts, stabilizers and linked
engines as flown. Survival time retains milliseconds; score and time have no
upper ceiling. The leaderboard keeps one best score per player and ship.
Only the new `ion-storm` challenge accepts writes. Earlier boards remain stored.
The challenge covers the combined gameplay and tuning changes since 0.5.6.

The Hangar gains a compact header, shared ship tabs and revised part information.
Rendering and effects gain shared flight layers and plasma light. The Lab’s
design-system references now live together at `/lab/ds`.

All workspace packages are versioned 0.6.0.
