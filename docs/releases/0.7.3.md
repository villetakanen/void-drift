# 0.7.3 — Fires in the Sky

Switch automatic weapons on or off in Practice and after reaching the arena in
Derelicts or Gauntlet. The Flight Deck's new flaps keep weapons and other
flight controls close at hand. The Field Guide, Lab navigation and mount
planner also receive updates. All four workspace packages are versioned 0.7.3.

## Ranked compatibility

Compared with [0.7.2](0.7.2.md), ranked Survival trajectories, survival
conditions, scoring and eligibility are unchanged. The weapons toggle is not
available in Survival, and automatic weapons remain on by default. Existing
scores stay on `fires-in-the-sky`; `CHALLENGE` and Firestore rules are unchanged.
