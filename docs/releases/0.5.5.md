# 0.5.5 — Arcade title and immersive flight

The arcade title screen brings navigation into the menu, with an incoming Wasp
illustration and slower, circular title-system orbits. Hangar flight returns to
its source, and Credits has its own page.

Survival fills an adaptive game stage without the site header. Navigation lives
behind Pause and the completed-run screen. Portrait orientation and backgrounding
suspend flight, clear held controls and require deliberate Resume.

Life support uses 1.5 energy per second, restoring steering power to the starter
Wasp. The playable starter carries a Gravity coil in its left hull slot; the
menu illustration remains independently equipped without that coil.

Scores use the new `arrival-2` challenge because life-support demand and the
starter loadout changed. Earlier scores remain stored under their original
challenge. The countdown, interstellar arrival and accepted propulsion model
from 0.5.4 remain in use. No gravity or friction constants change.

This patch also retains pending development evidence, reconciles the 0.5.4
release history, and carries the game/Lab/factory specification organization
and removal of unused study and engine code. The unused directional-propulsion
experiment is preserved separately on `codex/archive-directional-propulsion`.
All workspace packages are versioned 0.5.5.
