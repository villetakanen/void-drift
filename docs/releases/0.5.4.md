# 0.5.4 — Propulsion rebalance and interstellar arrival

Survival begins with a three-second Android-green stencil countdown at the centre
of the screen. Each run gets a fresh seed and enters from a random point on the
system rim at speed 600, heading 37 degrees off the inward radial. A green speed
line behind the ship shows its approach and fades after launch.

The accepted propulsion model gives each assembled ship one powered cruise ceiling:
hull base speed plus all installed main-engine contributions. Hull bases are Scout
370, Wasp 420, Fighter 330, Corvette 240 and Fiddler 280. Engine contributions are
Light ion 8, Heavy ion 11, Fission 17 and Plasma 9. Existing momentum above the ceiling,
braking and steering remain available. The Hangar and Lab share these values.

The mass-law gravity model published in 0.5.3 remains in use. This release introduces
no further gravity tuning. Scores use the new `arrival-1` challenge so the changed
starts and propulsion are ranked separately from earlier rules.

Flight Lab and builder Test flight retain their existing start. Custom Survival
uses the counted arrival. Reduced motion keeps the three-second count without
ignition flicker. All workspace packages are versioned 0.5.4.
