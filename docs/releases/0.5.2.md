# 0.5.2 — Salvage and presentation updates

Version 0.5.2 brings automatic weapons and salvage encounters to fitted Test flights, a procedural orbital opening scene, shared cockpit glass materials, and revised Top Scores.

- Railguns fire with recoil and swept collisions; rotating lasers defend against debris. Derelicts break into hazardous fragments that provide collectible energy.
- The opening scene uses shared star and planet rendering, with a flight-start transition and reduced-motion support.
- Approved cockpit materials appear in the Hangar, Lab, and flight, with the gilded surface study retained.
- Top Scores shows up to 30 times and opens after a successful save. Run-complete actions and responsive button groups follow shared styles.
- All workspace packages are versioned 0.5.2.

Weapon balance and physical-device performance remain separate human-review concerns.
