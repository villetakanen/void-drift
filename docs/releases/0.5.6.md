# 0.5.6 — Hangar, Derelicts and Light Ion Cannon

The Hangar moves to `/hangar`, with a compact opening, title navigation and an
Experimental control marked “Not available yet”. Existing `/builder` links
redirect while retaining ship selection. Experimental remains a reserved control.

Practice flight supports safe, repeatable trials. Derelicts flight adds seeded
wreck arrivals, physical hull collisions and fragments, energy release and a
completed-run score. These encounter scores remain local to the run.

Light Ion Cannon is available in weapon-compatible fittings, with shared procedural
artwork, automatic forward acquisition and power-consuming fire in Derelicts.
The Lab retains its design studies and production encounter demonstrations.

The accepted standard Sun shares collection and exposure profiles between flight
and analysis, with HUD feedback from actual effects. Ranked survival scores move
to `arrival-3` because the Sun gameplay rules changed since 0.5.5. Earlier scores
remain stored under their original challenges.

All workspace packages are versioned 0.5.6. Automated release verification does
not establish human visual acceptance, balance or physical-phone performance.
