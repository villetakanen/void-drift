# 0.7.0 — Fires in the Sky

Plasma engines and plasma rifles join ranked Survival. Pirates and drifting
derelicts make combat and salvage part of the score: one point per whole second
alive, 20 per collected cell, 10 for destroying a derelict, 2 for a shard and
50 for a pirate. Destruction awards require the player's finishing weapon hit
or ram; hits alone and environmental finishes earn nothing. The final total,
including zero for a sub-second run without awards, can reach the leaderboard.

The first ranked pirate arrives 45 gameplay seconds after GO. Its death or
departure starts a 45–60 second replacement wait. Derelicts attempt arrival
every 5–40 gameplay seconds, with one living wreck at a time, independently of
pirates. Gauntlet is an unranked encounter with seven armed Scouts arriving
from the opening through the first minute.

Survival, Derelicts and Gauntlet share seeded yellow-family stars, Mercurial
and Habitable worlds. Practice returns to Mercury, Venus and The Cradle.
A prepared Milky Way sky gives each generated system a repeatable backdrop;
Practice keeps its fixed Sol sightline. Shared game fields and revised glow
composition carry that presentation into the game menus and Lab.

Settings offers the Combat HUD switch alongside the camera preference. The
Hangar links to Settings and identifies blocked launches as an unsanctioned
build with Playground off. The small Service ion drive is available for
Playground test flights; it is not a ranked part. All four workspace packages
are versioned 0.7.0.

## Ranked compatibility

The baseline is the last published release, [0.6.3](0.6.3.md), tag `v0.6.3`
at `cb98c6a1854ea6f1511b7f1c148dd21f6e90bc5e`, which used `ion-storm`.
Ranked Survival now changes star and planet physics, adds armed pirate
encounters, changes derelict scheduling and outcome scoring, and permits
ranked plasma fittings. These changes affect trajectories, survival, scoring
and eligibility, so old and new scores are not comparable.

The release uses `fires-in-the-sky` and its separate leaderboard. Firestore
rules retain `ion-storm` writes for released clients and add the new challenge,
including zero-score submissions. The unpublished rc.1 work is not a separate
published baseline; all changes in this release share `fires-in-the-sky`.
Sky, camera, Combat HUD and rendering changes are presentation changes and do
not themselves require a new ranked board.
