# 0.7.2 — Fires in the Sky

- **See how your run ended.** Ranked Survival leads with your score and time
  survived. The results screen shows why the run ended and marks unranked runs.
- **Sign a new personal best.** Enter your initials to save a new best for the
  ship you flew. A successful save opens Top Scores. If you didn't beat your
  best, the result shows the score to beat instead.
- **Retry with the same setup.** Start a fresh run with a new seed and the same
  ship, fittings, mode and Playground setting.

## Known issue

Turning your device to portrait or narrowing the window at game over can clear
unsaved initials or prevent Top Scores from opening after a successful save.
[Reproduction and findings](../specs/game/game-screen/rotate-findings.md).
