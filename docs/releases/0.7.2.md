# 0.7.2 — Fires in the Sky

- See your final score and what ended your run at a glance.
- Retry with the same ship, fittings and mode, or return to where you launched.
- Sign a new ranked personal best with three initials and save it to the
  leaderboard. When your existing best stands, the result tells you instead of
  asking you to submit again.

This patch also includes Lab catalog and design-system updates.

## Ranked compatibility

Compared with [0.7.1](0.7.1.md), this patch changes the end-of-run experience,
not ranked Survival trajectories, survival conditions, scoring or run
eligibility. The board still retains one best score per pilot and ship, and a
saved best is only replaced by a higher score. Existing scores stay on
`fires-in-the-sky`; the challenge identifier and Firestore rules are unchanged.
