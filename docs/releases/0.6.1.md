# 0.6.1 — Experimental Gauntlet

The Hangar offers an unranked experimental Gauntlet encounter with two charcoal
pirate Scouts. Both pursue and attack the player; shots can hit the other pirate.
The encounter continues after one pirate is destroyed, and victory requires both.
The shared purple countdown, pause and return to the Hangar remain available.

This is an early playable experiment, with no progression, waves or multiplayer.
Survival rules and the `ion-storm` leaderboard challenge are unchanged.
All workspace packages are versioned 0.6.1.
